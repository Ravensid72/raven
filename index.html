The Barong Tagalog and the Baro’t Saya are the official national costumes of the Philippines, representing a blend of indigenous pre-colonial styles and Spanish colonial influences. They are typically crafted from piña (pineapple fiber) or jusi (banana silk). Today, they serve as symbols of Filipino cultural pride and are worn at formal events, weddings, and cultural celebrations.

Folk dance is a traditional, regional dance form passed down through generations, such as Tinikling, Itik-Itik, and Pandanggo sa Ilaw. It reflects the everyday life, customs, and history of a local community.
